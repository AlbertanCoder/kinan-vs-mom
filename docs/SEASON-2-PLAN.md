# SEASON 2 PLAN — a game that goes up and down (agreed with Shad, 2026-11-10)

Shad: "the game right now feels very flat and boring ... we want to bring more life to it, more adventure, more fun,
more variety." Kinan should go up and down, not only left to right: climb ladders, drop down pipes and chutes, find
secret rooms, wall-jump up shafts, grab ledges, climb, swim, with the camera following him high up and deep down.

Shad answered 32 multiple-choice questions; this file is the record. Build in **small steps, one per session**, each
one tested, played by Shad, then pushed live ("small updates often"). Shad decides if a step feels right.

## The decisions
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2D or 2.5D | **Stay 2D**, but greatly improve graphics, feel and how levels work |
| When | The new **moves** (wall jump, ledge grab, climbing, swimming) start **after World 11**. Ladders, pipes/chutes/doors and secret rooms need no new skill, so they go into **Worlds 1-11 too** |
| Goal | All equally: movement that feels fun, exploring and secrets, variety of places |
| Level shape | Mostly left to right but **tall**: big climbs and deep dives, some levels mainly up or down |
| New moves | Wall jump, ledge grab, climbing (vines, ladders, rock, fences), swimming |
| Wall jump | **Very forgiving**: slow wall slide, tap JUMP to bounce |
| Learning the moves | **Sabumnim** teaches them (taekwondo story) |
| Story reason | **Both**: Glitchy's escape bent the worlds taller and deeper, and Sabumnim trains Kinan for them |
| Touch controls | **No new buttons**: JUMP near a wall wall-jumps; ledges and climbing happen on touch |
| Followers (pets, Robin, Shad) | **Poof back beside Kinan** when they fall behind; they give **small helps** (a boost up, stand on a button, show a hidden handhold) |
| Camera | Smooth follow in every direction, looking ahead; zooms out a little for big climbs and dives (Shad skipped this one: our pick) |
| Long falls | **Land lower, no harm**; only bottomless pits cost a heart |
| Underwater | **No air limit** |
| Level length | About 5-8 minutes, with checkpoints (our pick) |
| Places | Mountains and cliffs, underwater / ocean, tall buildings, caves and mines, and more themes |
| Secrets | The best ones (golden candles, secret exits) **way up high and deep down**; the main path stays easy |
| Puzzles | Switches and doors, water levels (raise / lower), friend teamwork, physics (crates, seesaws, ropes, boulders) |
| Bosses | **Tall arenas** to wall-jump and climb in, mixed with today's flat fights |
| World 12 (3D sneak peek) | **Keep it as a 3D bonus world** (built already); the new 2D era starts in the world after it (our pick) |
| Worlds 1-11 | Keep their levels; add hidden paths / secret rooms (ladders, pipes, chutes, doors), the juice upgrade, and **one optional challenge stop per world** that uses the new moves once learned |
| New moves in old levels | **Only in new levels** (old levels keep their rules so they play as designed) |
| Feel today | Shad wants all of it better: jumping / running, graphics, juice (dust, squash, sparkles, shake, sounds), levels too flat |
| Juice upgrade | **Everywhere**, Worlds 1-11 included |
| Pipes / chutes / doors lead to | A mix: underground areas, rooftops, secret rooms, shortcuts. Themed per world, not copied from another game (laundry chute at home, vents at school, oil pipes at Dad's rig, manholes in Toronto) |
| Secret rooms hold | Treasure, mini challenges, story surprises (family memories, funny scenes, Easter eggs), shortcuts |
| More life | Things to ride (elevators, zip lines, minecarts, rafts, skateboard), living worlds (people and animals, weather, day and night), interactive stuff (trampolines, breakable walls, buttons, hiding spots, helpers), mini-games inside levels |
| Prototype | A **playground** test level first |
| Playtest | Shad plays each step |
| Pace | Small steps, one per session |

## Build order
**Phase A — the whole game feels alive (Worlds 1-11 too)**
1. Engine groundwork: taller levels, a camera that follows up and down, doors / pipes / chutes into other areas, and a
   playground test level (developer-only).
2. Ladders, pipes, chutes and secret rooms (treasure, mini challenge, story surprise, shortcut).
3. Better running and jumping, and the juice effects, in every world.
4. Worlds 1-11, one world per session: hidden paths and secret rooms that fit each place.
5. Things to ride and play with; living backgrounds.

**Phase B — the new era after World 11**
6. Wall jump, ledge grab, climbing, swimming; Sabumnim's training scene.
7. Puzzles: switches and doors, water levels, friend teamwork, crates and ropes.
8. New worlds (mountains, ocean, towers, caves), tall boss arenas, mini-games; a challenge stop on each old world's map.

## The Builder gets all of it (Shad, 2026-11-10)
Menu › More games › Builder gets a massive upgrade alongside the game: **everything Season 2 adds to the game also comes to
the Builder** (tall and deep levels, ladders, pipes / chutes / doors, secret rooms, rides, wall jumps, climbing, water ...).
The rule: **you can only build what Kinan has learned in the game.** A piece unlocks in the Builder once the player has
met it or learned it in the story (no wall-jump walls before Sabumnim teaches the wall jump, no deep levels before the
first deep level). The Builder must stay **super simple to use, with tutorials**, and also be **powerful enough to make
any level or world** Shad or Kinan want. Shad's Builder answers:

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Who it's for | **Kinan (11)**: big buttons, simple words, fun first |
| Unlock rule | Pieces not learned yet show **locked, with a hint** ("Learn the wall jump in World 12 first") |
| Teaching | A first-time **guided build** with a friend, a **30-second lesson per new piece**, **example levels** to open and pull apart, and the ❓ help on every tool |
| Size | **Same as the real game** (as long, and as tall / deep, as story levels) |
| Worlds | He can build **whole worlds with his own world map** (several levels, a castle at the end), played on the **Builder's own map**, separate from the story |
| Sharing | A **share code / QR code** (like Move my save; no accounts, nothing online) |
| Characters | **Bosses he has befriended** (in a boss arena), **friends and pets** that follow and help, **talking characters** with lines he types |
| Placing big pieces | **Drag and connect**: place a pipe, drag a line to where it leads; drag a box to fill water |
| Timing | **Right alongside the game**: each session that adds a piece to the game adds it to the Builder too |
| Helpers | A **"Can I beat it?" robot check**, **friendly warnings** (no start flag, a pit with no way out), **auto-decorate** |
| Drawing | **Pixel-art blocks**, **his own enemies / characters** (draw it, pick how it moves), **backgrounds** |
| Controls | **Big and simple**: one finger to place, two to scroll and zoom, easy undo |
| Rewards | **Small rewards**: a lesson or his first level earns a hat or candles for the Party Shop |
| Music | **Any song he has heard in the story** so far |
| Must not change | **His saved levels** all still open and play; **today's tools stay where they are** (new ones go around them) |

## Shad's playground feedback (2026-11-10) and his answers
He played the playground. **Fix:** on a ladder or vine Kinan faces it (seen from **behind**, arms and legs moving), not
sideways; the wall jump shows a **taekwondo kick-off** (foot on the wall, dust, a thud); the ledge grab stays but must
**look good** (hands on the edge, body against the wall, legs dangling, a clear pull-up); the **butt slam is slower and
floaty underwater** (bubbles; still breaks crates); **swim strokes** (arms and legs, bubbles; floats when idle);
**sounds** for climbing (creaks, rustles), water (splash, blub, bubbles), pipes and doors (whoomp, creak, clunk), the wall
kick (thud) and the ledge (click, hup). The playground had no sound: fix. The water and the secret doors look good.
**Next, one at a time after the polish (in this order):** crawl through tunnels first, then rope swing / monkey bars,
the taekwondo dash kick, glide. **Tools**, found in the level that needs them and gone at its end: a flashlight for dark
caves, a grappling hook, a shovel, a skateboard / scooter. **Climbing extras:** climbable rock walls / fences (up, down,
sideways), shimmying along a ledge while hanging, holding run to climb faster.

## The adventure test level (Shad, 2026-11-10)
Shad wants one long, real test level (not on the map) that he and his girlfriend can play for feedback: everything new
together, enemies and two bosses. If it works, all of it becomes the new era after World 11 (World 12 on, or 13-14 so the
World 12 sneak peek stays). His answers: keep the **ledge hang in a few spots** (it saves near-miss jumps); **Mountains to
the Sea** (forest vines, a cliff climb, an old mine with pipes and chutes, a waterfall, the ocean); **medium-hard but
fair** (checkpoints often, no cheap deaths); **about 6-8 minutes in 4 acts**; the **mid boss** a giant **mole** in a tall
mine arena (it pops up between three levels: climb and wall-jump to reach it); the **final boss** a **rising tide**: climb,
wall-jump and swim up a sea cave as it chases you, then calm the creature at the top; **bosses follow the game's rules**
(dumb then smarter, every attack shown first, calmed not beaten, no game over); **the game's foes plus a few new ones**
(a fish, a bat); **light story** (Sabumnim, Glitchy, short boss lines); **candles, 3 secrets and a golden candle**; an
**end-of-level card** (time, hearts lost, falls, secrets, deaths per act) to screenshot; its **own link** (?lab=adventure).
