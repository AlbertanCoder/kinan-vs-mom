# WORLDS 12-15 — AROUND THE WORLD (draft for Shad, 2026-11-10)

The original 22-world storyline (Worlds 1-11 across Canada, then a different country every world) lives in Shad's own
files and was not found in the repo or his Google Drive. Until it turns up, this plan follows what Shad remembers and
answered (docs/SEASON-2-PLAN.md): after World 11 every world is its own country, Glitchy is pulled across the world and
Kinan follows; India (Annie's home) and Japan were in the original; Shad picked Mexico and France. If the original is
found, it wins and this plan is changed to match.

## The story so far, and the turn
Worlds 1-11: Glitchy bursts out of Shad's present and runs across Canada; Kinan chases it world by world; in World 11
(Inside the Console) Glitchy's Last Stand ends with Glitchy becoming Kinan's friend.
**The turn (end of World 11, replaces the 2D-to-3D change):** the broken console sparks once more and opens a **world
portal**. It pulls Glitchy in. Glitchy's voice comes from far away ("KINAN! IT'S HOT HERE... AND THERE ARE ELEPHANTS?!").
**Annie** knows those sounds: India, her family's home. The family packs, and the map's last road ends at an **airport**.
From then on: **the portal keeps hopping**. In each country Glitchy is stuck somewhere famous and the portal's glitch bends
the place taller and deeper (Season 2's rule, docs/SEASON-2-PLAN.md). Kinan follows him country to country. At each castle
Kinan frees Glitchy, the portal yanks him away again, a clue says where. **World 15 (France):** Kinan reaches the
portal's core at the top of Paris, frees Glitchy for good and closes it. They fly home; a teaser points at the next 7
worlds (16-22).
**Who travels:** Kinan, Glitchy (when free), **Annie** (she joins in India and stays for the trip), the **cat and dog**
(always both). Sabumnim talks over video call and teaches each world's new move.
**Travel on the map:** a **plane route**: the airport stop at the end of each world, the plane flies over the sea to the
next country's map (like the bus and boat road trips).

## The rules every new world follows
- 11 stops: 8 levels, a fort, a secret, a castle. **1-2 chases** per world. **Bosses: fair but real** (4 stomps, quicker
  each time, every attack shown first, calmed not beaten). Local legend creatures, never people. Pets walk Kinan's path.
- **One new move per world**, taught by Sabumnim at the world's start, plus **one level tool**.
- **Famous places** as levels or backdrops, **food and festivals**: the candles become a local treat, festival lights
  in the backdrops. Respectful: places of worship are backdrops only (never somewhere enemies or fights happen).
- The Builder gets each move and piece as Kinan learns it.

## World 12 — INDIA (with Annie)
Move: **CRAWL** (duck into tight tunnels: alleys, vents, under market stalls). Tool: **flashlight** (the stepwell, caves).
Candles become **ladoos**. Backdrops: Diwali lights, kites over the rooftops. Chase: **the monsoon flood**.
1. 12-1 ANNIE'S VILLAGE (Punjab): wheat fields, a tractor ride, Annie's family; Sabumnim teaches the crawl.
2. 12-2 AMRITSAR STREETS: crowded lanes, crawl under stalls, the Golden Temple shining far behind (backdrop only).
3. 12-3 THE OLD DELHI BAZAAR: rooftops and wires, ladders between buildings, kites, a crawl through spice-sack tunnels.
4. 12-4 THE PINK CITY (Jaipur): tall pink walls, wall-jump chimneys, palace windows.
5. FORT: AMBER FORT — the **Market Monkey Boss**, a big cheeky monkey that steals Kinan's ladoos (tall arena, ropes of
   flags).
6. 12-5 THE STEPWELL: a deep step-well going **down** 4 screens, the flashlight, secret crawl spaces.
7. 12-6 KERALA BACKWATERS: houseboats, swimming, water foes.
8. 12-7 THE TIGER RESERVE (Sundarbans): mangrove vines, a gentle tiger chase (it just wants to play).
9. SECRET: THE TAJ AT MOONLIGHT (bonus: candles in the garden pools, the Taj Mahal backdrop).
10. 12-8 MUMBAI MONSOON: **flood chase** up the city's rooftops.
11. CASTLE: THE GATEWAY — the **Monsoon Frog**, a giant frog in a flooding arena (rising water, lily pads); free Glitchy;
    the portal yanks him to Japan.

## World 13 — JAPAN
Move: **ROPES AND MONKEY BARS** (swing on ropes over gaps, go hand over hand along bars). Tool: **grappling hook**.
Candles become **dango**. Backdrops: cherry blossoms, lanterns, Tokyo neon. Chase: **the Mount Fuji avalanche**.
1. 13-1 TOKYO NEON: Shibuya crossing, signs, rooftops; Sabumnim teaches ropes.
2. 13-2 THE BULLET TRAIN: run along the train roofs (ropes between cars).
3. 13-3 KYOTO BAMBOO FOREST: bamboo ropes and monkey bars, a tall green climb.
4. 13-4 THE THOUSAND GATES: red gates up a mountain path (backdrop: the shrine), foxes.
5. FORT: OSAKA CASTLE — the **Snow Monkey**, from the hot springs, throwing snowballs (tall arena).
6. 13-5 THE ROBOT CAFE: a tech level, conveyor belts, pipes.
7. 13-6 THE KOI POND GARDENS: swimming, bridges, lanterns.
8. 13-7 THE HOT SPRINGS VALLEY: steam vents lift Kinan, snow, monkeys.
9. SECRET: CHERRY BLOSSOM NIGHT (bonus).
10. 13-8 MOUNT FUJI: the **avalanche chase** down and across the mountain.
11. CASTLE: THE TANUKI'S TEMPLE GROUNDS — the **Big Tanuki** (a raccoon-dog from the old stories, a shape-changer);
    free Glitchy; the portal yanks him over the Pacific to Mexico.

## World 14 — MEXICO
Move: **DASH KICK** (a taekwondo flying kick through the air: crosses gaps, breaks cracked walls). Tool: **shovel** (dig
soft ground for buried candles and tunnels). Candles become **churros**. Backdrops: papel picado banners, Day of the
Dead marigolds, alebrije colours. Chase: **rising lava** in the volcano.
1. 14-1 MEXICO CITY MARKET: colours, piñatas; Sabumnim teaches the dash kick.
2. 14-2 THE PYRAMID OF THE SUN (Teotihuacan): a tall climb, dash kicks across broken steps.
3. 14-3 XOCHIMILCO CANALS: boat rides, swimming, flower boats.
4. 14-4 THE DIG SITE: the shovel, tunnels, old carvings.
5. FORT: THE JUNGLE TEMPLE — the **Giant Axolotl**, in a flooded temple arena.
6. 14-5 THE CENOTES (Yucatan): deep swimming caves, light shafts, the flashlight again.
7. 14-6 DAY OF THE DEAD PARADE: a fun, colourful night parade (marigolds, music), floats to ride.
8. 14-7 THE CACTUS DESERT: wind, tumbleweeds, a road-runner race.
9. SECRET: CHICHEN ITZA AT DAWN (bonus).
10. 14-8 THE VOLCANO: the **rising lava chase** up the crater.
11. CASTLE: THE CRATER'S HEART — the **Lava Iguana**; free Glitchy; the portal yanks him over the Atlantic to France.

## World 15 — FRANCE (the Season 2 finale)
Move: **GLIDE** (a jacket that opens like a kite: glide down from high places, steer, ride updrafts). Tool:
**skateboard** (fast streets, ramps). Candles become **croissants** (and macarons for golden ones). Backdrops: Paris
lights, lavender fields. Chase: **something big chasing** up the tower and through the sewers.
1. 15-1 PARIS STREETS: cafés, rooftops, the skateboard; Sabumnim teaches the glide.
2. 15-2 THE SEINE: boats, bridges, swimming under the arches.
3. 15-3 THE CATACOMB TUNNELS: crawl spaces, the flashlight, pipes (spooky but friendly).
4. 15-4 THE PALACE GARDENS (Versailles): hedge maze, fountains, glide over the gardens.
5. FORT: THE TOWER OF PIGEONS — the **Pigeon King**, a giant pigeon in a rooftop arena.
6. 15-5 THE LAVENDER FIELDS: glide on the warm wind, bees, windmills.
7. 15-6 MONT-SAINT-MICHEL: the sea comes in fast round the island (a **tide chase** up the abbey's island town).
8. 15-7 THE FRENCH ALPS: gliding from peak to peak, ski lifts.
9. SECRET: THE ART MUSEUM AFTER DARK (bonus: paintings come to life).
10. 15-8 THE EIFFEL TOWER: the big climb, **the Gargoyle chases** Kinan up the tower.
11. CASTLE: THE PORTAL CORE — at the tower's top: the **Stone Gargoyle** calmed, then the portal's core: every move
    Kinan learned (crawl, ropes, dash kick, glide, wall jump, swim) in one last climb; Glitchy freed for good; the
    portal closes. The family flies home; a teaser for Worlds 16-22.

- 14: the dash kick (a dash in the air, breaks cracked walls); the shovel (soft-dirt tiles); lava (the rise, with lava).
