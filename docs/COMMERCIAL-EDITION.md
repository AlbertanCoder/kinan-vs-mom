# The paid edition (planning notes, 2026-11-14)

Two versions from one codebase: **Kinan vs. Mom** stays the family's own game on its own link; a **paid edition** swaps
the names, faces, story text, voices, title and some places (a build switch), so fixes reach both.

## Who made it (Shad's answers)
- Shad is Kinan's big brother (not his dad).
- The store line tells the true story with **no names**: no one in the family is named on the store or in the paid game.
- It appears on the **store page** and on a small **About screen** in the game; nothing about it during play.
- The sentence: **"Made by a big brother as a birthday present for his little brother, then shared with every kid who
  loves an adventure."**

## Must change before selling (from the 2026-11-14 check; have a lawyer look before launch)
- **Voices:** all clips were made with Microsoft's free read-aloud voices (edge-tts), which are not licensed for selling:
  re-make them with a paid service that allows commercial use (Microsoft Azure Speech: the same voices, a few dollars
  for the whole game) or voice actors. The renamed characters need new recordings anyway.
- **Real people:** Kinan, Mom, Robin, Chenoa, Shad, Annie, Grandpa, Sabumnim and the real pets get new names, looks and
  personalities.
- **Places:** public places can stay; swap anything with a business name (hotels, shops) and anything private.
- **Ko-fi link:** out of the paid edition (kids' app rules: no links out without a parent check).
- Already fine: the art and music (made by the game's code), the fonts (Nunito, Pixelify Sans: open licence), three.js
  (MIT: keep its licence file), no accounts, ads or tracking.

## Selling
- Pay once (about $2.99-4.99), or free Worlds 1-3 and one unlock behind a parent check. No ads, no loot boxes.
- First: itch.io (web, as it is). Then phones (Capacitor; Apple $99 a year, Google $25 once; a privacy policy, an age
  rating, the kids' app rules), later Steam.
- Before charging: a clear name and one-line hook, a polished first 10-15 minutes, great phone controls, fewer and
  better voice lines, a trailer and screenshots.
