# KINAN vs. WORLDS — THE STORY, WORLD 1 TO WORLD 22 (Shad, 2026-11-10)

Shad's original 22-world storyline was not found (not in the repo, Work-Station or Google Drive). This bible keeps every
story beat already in the game (Worlds 1-11) and continues it from Shad's answers. If the original turns up, it wins and
this is changed to match. Every new line of story must agree with this page.

## The family (the team)
- **Kinan** — turning 11; taekwondo student (belts from Sabumnim); brave, funny, a kid (grossed out by mushy stuff).
- **Mom** — protective ("Kinan, stay close!"); joins the team at Niagara Falls (World 10).
- **Dad (Robin)** — dad jokes everywhere; oilfield worker (World 3).
- **Big Sis Chenoa** — the artist (her art room, 1-7); calls Kinan "little bro".
- **Big Bro Shad** — the builder (hard hat, hammer); his present started everything; his console holds the realms.
- **Annie** — the tech whiz with scanners, drones and firewalls. **She grew up in India** and moved to Canada; she lives in
  **Montreal**, where she met the family (World 9). Speaks Punjabi ("Chalo!", "Hai Rabba!"). **Japan is her dream:** she is
  the biggest Japan fan ever (anime, manga, history, food, kimono), a walking history book there (Amandeep's wish).
- **Glitchy** — the purple one-eyed glitch from Shad's present; Kinan's friend from the end of World 11.
- **Sabumnim** — Kinan's calm taekwondo instructor; teaches by video call; gives the belts.
- The pets (always a cat and a dog along), the grandmas, Grandpa, and the friends made on the way.

## SEASON 1 — BIRTHDAY WEEK ACROSS CANADA (Worlds 1-11, built)
It's Kinan's 11th birthday (World 1 is the day itself; the trip is his birthday week). A present from Shad says "Don't open
this until I get there!" Kinan peeks; **Glitchy** bursts out, zips past Mom and out of the window, and runs across Canada.
Kinan chases him one world at a time; at every castle Glitchy escapes into the next world.
1 Saskatoon (home, Mom) · 2 School · 3 Dad's oilfield · 4 Edmonton · 5 Calgary Stampede · 6 Drumheller · 7 Vancouver ·
8 Toronto · 9 Montreal (Annie, her firewall; she joins) · 10 Niagara Falls (Mom's last inspection; Mom joins; THE TURN) ·
11 Inside the Console (Shad's unfinished realms; Glitchy's Last Stand; Glitchy becomes a friend).

### The new end of World 11 (replaces the 3D unfold; Shad: 2D only)
Glitchy, calm at last: "Fr... friends?" The title glitches from KINAN vs. MOM to **KINAN vs. WORLDS**. The console lets them
go and everyone lands **home in Saskatoon** (2D). That evening: **Kinan's birthday party at the Bessborough** (the castle
hotel by the river) — the family, the friends made on the way, the pets, cake, the memory gifts. Season 1 ends happily.
**Then Shad's last present:** "One more, bud. And this time... wait till I say so." A small **glowing globe**. Kinan
doesn't touch it — but **Glitchy** does (the same "don't open it yet!" joke as World 1). The globe flashes, a **portal**
opens, and Glitchy is pulled inside. His voice comes from far away. **Annie** goes pale: those sounds, the smells, the
music — "That's... home. That's India." The family looks at each other. **Shad:** "Pack your bags. We're going to get him."
TO BE CONTINUED. (The 3D Saskatoon levels come off the map.)

## SEASON 2 — AROUND THE WORLD (Worlds 12-15, to build; levels: docs/WORLDS-12-15-PLAN.md)
The globe's portal hops from country to country, and its glitch bends each place taller and deeper (why Season 2 levels go
up and down). **The whole family flies after Glitchy** (a plane route on the map from each world's airport). In each
country Glitchy is stuck somewhere famous; at each castle Kinan frees him, the portal yanks him away again, a clue says
where. Each country, Sabumnim teaches one new move over a **video call**, and Glitchy learns **a few new words** (a word
from that country's language each time — funny and sweet).
- **World 12 — INDIA, Annie's home.** Annie grew up here; she shows everyone around (her old streets, her family's
  village in Punjab, the food), proud and happy. Move: crawl. Chase: the Mumbai monsoon flood. Castle: the Monsoon Frog.
  Glitchy's word: "Namaste!" The portal pulls him east.
- **World 13 — JAPAN, Annie's dream.** Annie is beside herself — the happiest she is all game: polite Japanese words she
  learned, sushi (the candles are sushi here), anime, the history (the Shinkansen of 1964, Osaka Castle of 1583, kitsune,
  hanami, onsen), and she wears a rented kimono at the Thousand Gates. Move: ropes and monkey bars. Chase: the Mount Fuji
  avalanche. Castle: Himeji, the White Heron Castle: an old suit of SAMURAI ARMOUR the glitch woke up (a thing, not a
  person); calmed, it bows, and Annie tells Kinan to bow back, the way samurai thanked each other after a match. Glitchy:
  "Arigato!" The portal pulls him over the Pacific.
- **World 14 — MEXICO, Shad's world.** Shad has always wanted to come: the food (churros, tacos, elote, tamales), the
  music and colours, the history (he tells Kinan about the pyramids and the old cities). Move: dash kick. Chase: rising
  lava in the volcano. Castle: the Lava Iguana. Glitchy: "¡Hola, amigos!" The portal pulls him over the Atlantic.
- **World 15 — FRANCE, the finale.** Paris: **Mom and Dad get lovey-dovey about "the city of love"**, Robin cracks jokes
  about it, and Kinan is totally grossed out ("Come ON, you guys, STOP!") — a running gag all world. **Chenoa** shines in
  the art museum (the secret level where the paintings come alive). Move: glide. Chase: the Gargoyle up the Eiffel Tower.
  Castle: the Portal Core at the top of the tower — every move Kinan learned in one last climb; **Glitchy freed for
  good**; the portal closes. Glitchy: "Merci... famille." (his best words yet). They fly home together.
**The Season 2 ending:** home again; the globe sits quiet on the shelf... then one by one, new countries **glow** on it. A
hint the trip isn't over (no villain — just more adventure). TO BE CONTINUED in World 16.

## SEASON 3 — THE GLOBE GLOWS AGAIN (Worlds 16-22, outline only: decide with Shad before building)
The globe wakes again; each glowing country is a new world, with the same rules (a country per world, a new move or tool,
a chase, local legend bosses, the family). Countries and the season's ending to be chosen with Shad (his original plan may
name them). Kinan turns into a black belt along the way (the belts continue).

## Rules for writing story lines
- Short lines (one breath), a kid's sense of humour, kind; nobody is beaten up (bosses are calmed and become friends).
- Each family member keeps their voice: Robin's dad jokes, Mom's "stay close", Chenoa's "little bro", Shad's "bud",
  Annie's tech talk and Punjabi words, Glitchy's beeps and few words.
- Countries are shown with warmth and respect: people, food, places, history; places of worship only as backdrops.
- Real-life feelings Shad shared stay as the characters' (Annie loves Japan, Shad loves Mexico); no romance for them.
